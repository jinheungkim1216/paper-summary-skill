# Domain supplement — physics (non-HEP)

Write a **도메인 보충: physics** section. Adapt to whether the paper is
theoretical, computational/simulation, or experimental. Quote numbers and
uncertainties verbatim with reference tags.

## Theory / framework
- **Physical setting & regime** — system, scales, units, limits/approximations
  (and where they break down).
- **Governing equations & assumptions** — key equations, symmetries,
  conservation laws, boundary/initial conditions.
- **Derivation logic** — the central steps; what is exact vs. perturbative/
  phenomenological.

## Methods (experiment / simulation)
- **Setup** — apparatus or numerical scheme; resolution/discretization;
  convergence checks.
- **Measurement** — observables, calibration, statistics; **uncertainties**
  (statistical vs. systematic) and significance.

## Results & validation
- **Key quantities** — central values with error bars.
- **Theory ↔ experiment/data comparison** — agreement, tensions, goodness-of-fit.
- **Predictions & testability** — falsifiable predictions, parameter ranges,
  follow-up experiments.
